About Me

I'm based in Karachi and currently work as an Import Coordinator at Mian Shafiq Business International. My day-to-day covers the full import process for direct and indirect materials: sourcing vendors, negotiating rates, booking sea and air freight, handling LC processing, and coordinating customs and port clearance. I stay in regular contact with overseas suppliers, banks, insurance companies, shipping lines and clearing agents, and I try to keep freight costs and demurrage under control before they become a problem. Most of this work runs through Odoo.

Alongside that, I also handle warranty and claims coordination for MTAX Group, a sister concern of Mian Shafiq in the textile industry that imports embroidery machinery. I liaise with suppliers on claims and after-sales service there. I'm currently finishing a BMS in Artificial Intelligence & Digital Media at Iqra University (2025–2028) while working full-time, and I've picked up certifications in Lean Thinking & Six Sigma, Sustainable Supply Chain, and a couple of AI workflow tools along the way. Right now I'm open to Import Operations, Import Executive or Supply Chain roles in Karachi.
