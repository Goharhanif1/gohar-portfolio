Environmental Science • Group Research

Air Quality Assessment & Health Correlations in Karachi (Korangi)

Environmental Studies & Sustainability • Iqra University • Team of 4 • Instructor: Ms Maryam Zehra

A comprehensive field study of air pollution across Korangi's residential and industrial zones, combining questionnaires, interviews, field observation, satellite imagery, and secondary data to link air quality with public health and biodiversity loss.

  • Primary data: Google Forms questionnaires filled by residents, workers, and shopkeepers, plus geo-tagged photography and interviews.
  • Tools: Google Earth Pro for mapping and temporal imagery (2001 vs 2025), Microsoft Word for analysis, and secondary sources such as IQAir, WHO, and Indus Hospital respiratory data.
  • Findings: Residential respondents rated pollution "Very Serious" (77.3%), citing garbage burning (36.8%) and construction dust (31.6%). Industrial respondents rated it "Serious" (66.7%). Both areas reported falling bird and fish numbers.
  • Scope: Comparative temporal analysis, SDG-wise adaptation measures, the London model of pollution control, and mitigation predictions.

Applied Mathematics • Research Paper

An Investigation Study of Algebraic Signal Processing

Co-author with Sir Haris Yaseen • Iqra University Main Campus, Karachi • 22 pages

A rigorous research paper tracing seven decades of signal-processing literature, from Shannon (1948) to 2021, analyzing how algebraic structures (polynomial rings, finite fields, and lattices) combine media compression and error correction into a single pipeline.

  • Key Contribution: A generalised ASP model unifying transform coding and error correction, algebraic transforms for sparse representation, and joint source-channel coding strategies.
  • Reported Results: Up to 20% higher compression efficiency and approximately 40% fewer retransmissions in wireless video compared to conventional layered approaches.
  • Scope: Image, video, and audio case studies, mathematical foundations, standardisation, and future directions.
